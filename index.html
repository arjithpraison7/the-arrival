About the Ecumenicals

One Saviour, one family, one evening of praise

Every Christmas, believers from churches of different traditions come together under one roof to celebrate the birth of our Lord Jesus Christ with carols, Scripture, prayer and fellowship. That coming together is what we call the Ecumenicals.

This year the Youth Fellowship of CSI All Saints' Church, Triplicane, hosts the evening under the theme “The Arrival”: the arrival of Yeshua, Emmanuel, God with us. Whatever church you call home, you are warmly welcome. Bring your family and friends.
